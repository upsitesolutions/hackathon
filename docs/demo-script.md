# Sous — Demo Script (3 min)

**Presenter:** Matt  
**Device:** Physical iOS device (not simulator)  
**Photos pre-loaded:** none — load live during demo  
**Recipe pre-staged:** French onion soup (seeded in Cosmos DB)

---

## Timing overview

| Segment | Time | Running total |
|---------|------|---------------|
| The problem | 30s | 0:30 |
| Sous catches it (onions) | 90s | 2:00 |
| Rescue mode (steak) | 45s | 2:45 |
| Close | 15s | 3:00 |

---

## Script

### 0:00 — The problem (30s)

> *Open Sous to the home screen. Hold phone so judges can see.*

**SAY:** "You're making French onion soup. You pull up a recipe. Step 4 says — *caramelize onions, 45 minutes, medium-low heat.*"

> *Pause. Let it land.*

**SAY:** "How do you know when they're done? Not burned — done. The recipe can't tell you. It never could. That's the gap Sous closes."

---

### 0:30 — Sous catches it (90s)

> *Tap into the French onion soup recipe. Navigate to the Caramelize step.*

**SAY:** "I'm on the Caramelize step. I'll show Sous what my onions look like right now."

> *Tap the camera button. Load `onions-caramelize-early.jpg` (pale gold, just softened).*

**SAY:** "Still pale. Is that fine?"

> *Wait for verdict card to appear.*

**SAY:** "Adjust — *not there yet. Look for deep amber, jammy texture. Keep going on medium-low.*"

> *Pause for judges to read the card.*

**SAY:** "Now watch this. Same step, same recipe — thirty minutes later."

> *Tap camera again. Load `onions-caramelize-done.jpg` (deep amber, jammy).*

**SAY:** "Done."

> *Wait for verdict. Let the card speak.*

**SAY:** "That's the moment. Sous didn't just check — it told you exactly what to look for, and confirmed when you hit it. No guessing. No ruined soup."

---

### 2:00 — Rescue mode (45s)

> *Navigate back to recipe list. Tap into a steak recipe (or say "different recipe" and tap into steak). Navigate to the Sear step.*

**SAY:** "Same idea — different recipe. I just put my steak in the pan."

> *Load `steak-sear-bad.jpg` (pale grey, wet, no crust).*

> *Wait for verdict.*

**SAY:** "Adjust — *pan wasn't hot enough. That grey color means no Maillard reaction. Pull the steak, raise the heat to smoking, then re-sear.*"

> *Pause.*

**SAY:** "Every cooking mistake has a visual signature. Sous reads it."

---

### 2:45 — Close (15s)

> *Lock screen or set phone face-down. Look up.*

**SAY:** "Recipes tell you what to do. Sous tells you if you're doing it right. That's the gap — and that's what we built."

---

## If you run long

Cut the steak rescue entirely — the onion sequence is the core demo. End at 2:00 with:  
> "Every cooking mistake has a visual signature. Sous reads it. Recipes never could."

## If the verdict takes >5s to load

Don't fill silence with filler. Say: *"The model is reasoning about it..."* — makes it feel deliberate.

## Reset between runs

- Clear photo from camera picker (don't let it auto-load the last one)
- Collapse verdict card before handing phone to a judge
